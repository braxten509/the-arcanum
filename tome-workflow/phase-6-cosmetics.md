# Phase 6 — Cosmetics

Read **§2 `[[themes]]`, `[[shop]]`, and `[[badges]]`**. Create 3–5 distinct palettes,
one earned, plus theme shop entries, the engine consumables reflavored to this course,
and the complete badge bank in the mentor's voice.

Design every palette rather than renaming scaffold colors. Keep the candlelit parchment
study recognizable: `bg0` reads as wood, `bg1`–`bg3` as tinted paper, accents as ink, and
`candle` as a warm bare `r, g, b` triple. Vary paper, accent, candle, and sigil colors
enough that each palette has its own identity. The validator owns the exact 22-variable,
distance, reference, earned-theme, consumable, and badge-id contracts.

Produce `themes.toml`, `shop.toml`, and `badges.toml`. A tome recolors the existing study;
do not add CSS or anything under `skins/`.
